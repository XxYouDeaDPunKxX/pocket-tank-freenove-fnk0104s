Install Pocket Tank on Freenove FNK0104S

Pocket Tank beta for the Freenove FNK0104S 4.0-inch ESP32-S3 display, based on the original project by StratoBuilds.

Install

Pick your board.

  1. Plug the board into your computer. Use a USB-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.
  2. Click Install. Pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list. On Linux it may say ttyACM0.
  3. Wait a minute or two. When it says Installation complete, click Next.
  4. Connect it to Wi-Fi if desired. Pick your network and type its password, or skip and configure it later. The published beta checks for newer signed FNK0104S beta releases through this fork's OTA channel. Wi-Fi also synchronizes the clock.
    • New board: you get a fresh tank with two baby fish.
    • Already have a tank: it comes back just as you left it.

Updating over USB? Use the same button without selecting erase. It writes the firmware partitions without intentionally clearing your saved tank. Keeping a backup is prudent during beta testing.

The pop-up says Install even for USB updates. If the screen is dark, press BOOT once to wake it; in SCREEN sleep you can also touch the display.

Start over

To reset your tank, you don't need this page. Hold BOOT, tap the screen, and answer YES. You get two new baby fish.

Board not starting, or want a clean wipe? This button erases everything, then installs fresh. Your tank will be gone.

In the pop-up, tick Erase device. If you leave it unticked, it just installs like the red button.

Which board?

Supported

Freenove FNK0104S, ESP32-S3 with 4.0-inch ST7796 480x320 touch display. Requires USB-C power.

This installer is for the FNK0104S only. Confirm your board model before flashing; other devices are unsupported.

Having trouble?

My board isn't in the list

If the screen is dark, the tank is asleep. Press PWR firmly to wake it, then try again.

Still missing? Hold BOOT while you plug in a data cable to enter ESP32-S3 download mode, then select its serial port.

Also try another cable, and plug straight into the computer, not a hub.

The install fails or stops partway

Close anything else using the board, like a serial monitor or this page in another tab. Unplug, plug back in, and click Install again.

Still failing? Hold BOOT while you plug in, then try again. An interrupted flash may require retrying the installation.

Linux: "permission denied"

Run sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER, then log out and back in. (On some systems the group is uucp.)

Windows: it shows up but won't connect

Windows 10 and 11 already have the driver. If Device Manager shows a warning on the board, install Espressif's USB driver or try another USB port.

Back up my tank first (optional, uses a terminal)

Updates keep your tank, but if you'd like a copy: install esptool (pip install esptool) and wake the tank. To save it: esptool.py --chip esp32s3 read_flash 0x9000 0x6000 my-tank.bin. To put it back: esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x9000 my-tank.bin

What gets installed?

The signed app, the 7.56 MB AI model, bootloader, partition table, model trailer and OTA initialization data. The normal installer preserves the NVS save partition; the separate erase option clears saved state and Wi-Fi credentials.

The fish simulation and AI inference run on the board without a cloud AI service. The browser fetches the installer assets from this site's host; when you enable Wi-Fi, the board can contact time synchronization servers and this fork's update service. After it starts

A new tank opens with a quick setup: place the bubbles, then name your two fish and pick their colors. Then:

  • Feed: tap the water.
  • Say hi: hold a finger on the glass and they'll come over.
  • Clean: drag across the glass to wipe off algae.
  • Trim: swipe sideways through the grass.
  • Light: double-tap the glass.
  • Sleep: press BOOT; a touch wakes the screen in SCREEN mode. Other sleep modes may wake differently.
  • Reset: hold BOOT and tap the glass.
